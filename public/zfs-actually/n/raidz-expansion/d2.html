RAIDZ Expansion

definition
depth 1/2 − deeper + surface

Can you add one more disk to an existing RAIDZ team when you need more room? Two confident answers circulate: "of course—storage grows" (what store-bought boxes teach) and "never—ZFS locks the team size forever" (a decade of forum truth). Actually, both are stale: since early 2025 (OpenZFS version 2.3), you can "add new devices to an existing RAIDZ pool, increasing storage capacity without downtime" (release notes)—one command, while everything keeps running.

When reading ZFS advice, check the date. This feature made years of confident guidance obsolete overnight, and the forums have long memories.
