Snapshots, backups, and actually sleeping at night 5/6 ← prev next →

The naive slot for "replication tool" is rsync-shaped: walk the tree, compare, copy what changed. Actually, zfs send serializes a snapshot—an exact, block-level, point-in-time state of a dataset—into a stream, and zfs recv reconstructs it perfectly on any pool: every file, property, and the snapshot itself. No walking, no comparing, no "mostly the same." rsync approximates sameness file-by-file over a live, shifting target; recv is sameness, of a frozen moment.
