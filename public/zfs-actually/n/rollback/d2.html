Rollback

procedure
depth 1/3 − deeper + surface

Proxmox-style VM snapshots teach a seductive model: the timeline is a scrubber bar—jump to Tuesday, look around, jump forward again. Actually, zfs rollback is demolition, not navigation: making Tuesday the present destroys everything after Tuesday—the data since, and every later snapshot. Permanently. There is no roll forward, because forward no longer exists.

⚠ DESTRUCTIVE—verify before running. The -r flag doesn't protect later snapshots; it confirms their destruction (rollback to a non-latest snapshot refuses to run without it—the refusal is the safety, the flag disarms it). Semantics per zfs-rollback(8).
