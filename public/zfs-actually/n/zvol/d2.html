Zvols

definition
depth 1/3 − deeper + surface

A zvol is a slice of the pool that pretends to be a blank disk instead of a folder of files. You hand it to something that wants a whole disk of its own—most often a virtual machine (a computer simulated in software)— and that thing formats and uses it exactly as if it were real hardware.

The confusion it resolves: people go hunting for "the file" that holds their virtual machine's disk and find nothing—because there is no file. The virtual disk is carved straight from the pool. And underneath the pretense it's still ZFS: fingerprint-checked, freezable in time, copyable to another machine, like everything else in the pool.
