ZFS is the storage system that assumes your hardware is lying to you—and builds everything else from that one act of distrust. Most people meet it sideways, through Proxmox or TrueNAS or a homelab guide, and end up typing commands they don't own. This site is the mental model underneath the commands: what a pool actually is, why snapshots cost nothing until they cost everything, and where the real failure lines run. the OpenZFS documentation
