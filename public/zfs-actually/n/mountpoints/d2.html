Mountpoints

concept
depth 1/3 − deeper + surface

You go looking for how your datasets mount, open /etc/fstab, and find nothing. Decades of muscle memory say that file is the source of truth. Actually, ZFS mounts itself: the mountpoint is a property of the dataset, stored inside the pool, inherited down the tree. zfs set mountpoint=/srv/media tank/media is the entire edit; at import, ZFS mounts everything where its properties say it belongs.

The deeper shift: fstab describes storage to the OS. A ZFS pool carries its own description—the configuration lives with the data, not with the machine.
