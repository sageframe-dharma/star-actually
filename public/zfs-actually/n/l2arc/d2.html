Where the speed actually comes from 2/5 ← prev next →

The instinct: the pool is slow, there's a spare fast drive (an SSD) in the drawer, so add it as a cache—more cache is always better. Actually, L2ARC—a second-tier cache on that fast drive—is not free speed. Here's the catch: ZFS must keep a catalog in memory of everything parked on the cache drive. Every piece cached on the SSD spends a little of the memory cache—the one that's actually fast. On a machine without much memory, you can end up trading excellent cache for mediocre cache and calling it an upgrade.
