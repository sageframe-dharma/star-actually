The Pool

concept
depth 1/3 − deeper + surface
Build your first pool with intent 2/9 ← prev next →

The pool is what ZFS turns your disks into: one shared pot of storage that the whole machine draws from. You hand ZFS the disks; it hands you back a single supply of space. You never carve it up in advance, never decide sizes ahead of time, never "resize" anything again—everything on the machine simply shares the pot.

The surprise hiding inside the word: the pool itself keeps no spare copies of anything. Protection against a dead disk lives one level down, inside the small teams of disks the pool is built from (called vdevs—the next idea to meet). That distinction is the difference between a safe setup and a time bomb, which is why it gets its own page.
