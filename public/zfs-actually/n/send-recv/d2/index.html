Send and Receive

concept
depth 1/4 − deeper + surface
Snapshots, backups, and actually sleeping at night 5/6 ← prev next →

Send and receive are how a snapshot—a frozen moment of a dataset— travels to another machine. send turns the moment into a flowing copy; receive, on the other end, rebuilds it perfectly: every file, every setting, the snapshot itself. And after the first full trip, ZFS ships only what changed between one moment and the next.

The mental slot to avoid: this is not a file-syncing tool with extra steps. Sync tools (like rsync) hunt for differences—walking every folder, comparing files, approximating sameness against a moving target. ZFS doesn't hunt, because it already knows exactly what changed between two frozen moments. The copy isn't approximately the same; it is the same.
