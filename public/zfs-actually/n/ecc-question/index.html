The ECC Question

concept
depth 1/3 − deeper + surface

The claim arrives with total confidence: running ZFS on non-ECC RAM is reckless—a scrub with bad memory will "correct" your good data into garbage, pool-wide. It has the shape of expert knowledge: specific, counterintuitive, scary. Actually, here's Matt Ahrens, co-creator of ZFS: "There's nothing special about ZFS that requires/encourages the use of ECC RAM more so than any other filesystem." ECC is good engineering—for every filesystem equally. ZFS on consumer RAM is still safer than ext4 on the same RAM, because everything ZFS verifies is still being verified.
